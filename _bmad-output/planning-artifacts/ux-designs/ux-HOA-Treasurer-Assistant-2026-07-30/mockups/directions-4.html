Four visual directions — the Watchdog dashboard

The same screen, four complete personalities. Same content, same information architecture, same alert lifecycle. What differs is density, typographic register, and how status is encoded. Pick one, or name what you'd take from each.

A · Institutional

The register of record. Serif figures, hard rules, navy and brass — reads like something produced by an institution rather than an app. Status is a margin tick, not a colored pill. Most formal; risks feeling austere to a volunteer who isn't a finance person.

B · Plainspoken

Built for a volunteer, not an accountant. Large type, one idea per card, generous space, a single calm accent with warning reserved for genuine warnings. Lowest density here — it says two things need you, and what to do about them.
